Try a payment with test funds

Practice the full flow: choose who you intend to pay, check the receiving address, then approve in your wallet. Just want to compare addresses? Use Check an address—no funds or wallet connection needed.

Only the sending wallet needs test SOL first

For a SOL test, your wallet pays the amount plus a small network fee. The receiving address gets the test SOL and does not need advance funding.

Use free devnet funds, not real SOL. A brand-new receiving address needs a minimum transfer; we check that before asking you to sign.

Your wallet may add a priority fee. Doppel accepts at most 0.0001 test SOL in priority fees and 0.00012 test SOL in total network fees; the payment amount never changes. These limits apply only to free devnet test funds.
